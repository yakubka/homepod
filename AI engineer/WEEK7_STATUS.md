Semantics to remember:
- The segmenter never waits for a pause after the wake word. The rolling buffer is replayed on wake, so "Hey Jarvis turn on the lights" is one continuous utterance.
- Activation anchor: a speech region that ends at or before `activation_sample` is old conversation and is discarded; a region that is still speaking at the activation point, or starts after it, is kept. Speech joined to the wake phrase with a pause shorter than the end-silence setting is one region and is kept whole (documented limit).
- The no-speech timer starts at the activation sample, not at the start of the replayed pre-roll.
- Start: score >= start_threshold. Silence: score <= end_threshold. Real hysteresis: start_threshold > end_threshold.
- Every duration is rounded UP to whole samples once; decisions use integer samples.
- A gap or overlap in the sample index raises `AudioContinuityError`; audio is never silently joined.
- Decisions are taken at whole VAD-frame boundaries, so an entered duration is effectively rounded up to the next frame (25 samples with 10-sample frames means 30). This is frame granularity, not a numerical error.
- MAX_DURATION is measured from the padded segment start, so onset padding uses part of the maximum budget; the emitted PCM never exceeds max_utterance.
- At end of stream a final incomplete VAD frame is never padded: it is reported as `unscored_samples` and is excluded from the returned segment.
- TOO_SHORT ends the activated capture (a later command is not captured).

Task 4 facts to remember (2026-10-12):
- ONE master sample clock: sample 0 is the first sample fed after the pipeline is built or `restart_stream()` is called; it counts real samples only. The rolling buffer, the segmenter and every segment index use it.
- The wake detector has its own clock (samples since ITS last reset). The pipeline owns the detector and maps `master = detector_origin + Detection.end_sample`; it is never passed through unmapped, and a detector touched behind the pipeline's back raises `PipelineClockError`.
- A detection that falls on the zero-padded last chunk of a finite file is CLAMPED to the last real sample (`activation_clamped=True`); the padding never reaches the buffer or the segmenter. Because the frozen segmenter treats speech ending at or before the activation as old, such a wake yields NO_SPEECH. Any other activation outside the delivered PCM is INVALID_ACTIVATION.
- Statuses: TRANSCRIBED, WAKE_ONLY, NO_SPEECH, TOO_SHORT, MAX_DURATION, STREAM_ENDED, STT_EMPTY, STT_FAILED, INVALID_ACTIVATION. Only TRANSCRIBED is a complete command; MAX_DURATION and STREAM_ENDED carry the text of a CUT segment. STT is called exactly once per completed segment and never otherwise.
- Wake-prefix normalisation removes one configured phrase from the LEADING edge only (case-insensitive, whole words, punctuation tolerant) and nothing else. An internal "jarvis" is never touched; "Hey Jarvis" alone is WAKE_ONLY.
- The result holds no PCM and hides the transcript from `repr`; logs carry structure only. Every result is labelled SOFTWARE-VALIDATED and cannot be built with another label.
- TOO_SHORT ends the activation and the pipeline returns to idle: the user must say the wake word again (UX limitation, deferred to the product state machine).
- LIVE CONTINUITY IS STILL NOT VALIDATED: the pipeline refuses a gap or overlap only when the caller supplies `start_sample`; a block dropped silently by `audio/microphone.py` is invisible to it.
